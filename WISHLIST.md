## Guiding Principles

As LunaCycle develops, new features should be evaluated against several principles:

**Evidence over assumptions.**

Use credible sources and clearly communicate uncertainty.

**Education over diagnosis.**

Help users understand their health without pretending to replace a healthcare professional.

**Transparency over simplification.**

If research is uncertain, say so.

**User control over data.**

Users should decide what personal information is stored, used, changed, or deleted.

**Accessibility over complexity.**

Medical information should be understandable without requiring specialized knowledge.

**Awareness over stigma.**

Menstrual and reproductive health should be treated as legitimate areas of science and healthcare.

**Innovation with responsibility.**

New technology should improve access to information without sacrificing privacy, safety, or scientific accuracy.
